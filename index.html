Frequently Asked Questions

Are you available for freelance web development in Karachi?

Yes, I am a professional freelance web developer operating out of Karachi, Pakistan. I work with local companies and startups in Clifton, DHA, and across Sindh, as well as providing premium front-end web design and development services for clients globally.

Do you design dental clinic and dentist websites?

Yes, custom dentist website development is one of my core niches. I build highly responsive clinic websites (like AuraSmile and Dental Wellness) equipped with custom service lists, dentist credentials, client reviews, and direct WhatsApp integrations to simplify patient appointment scheduling.

What features do you build for gym and fitness center websites?

For gym websites (like Travis Gym and Ironcore Gym), I deliver high-contrast, premium dark layouts presenting membership pricing packages, classes schedules, coach/trainer showcases, and contact inquiry forms designed to turn website visitors into active members.

How do you ensure fast page speeds and optimization?

Every project I build is fully hand-coded using optimized semantic HTML, modular CSS transitions, and deferred Javascript loaders. I convert all image assets to highly compressed WebP formats and embed Schema.org structured metadata ready for top Google Search crawl rankings.
